# The Garden: product and research charter

This is the repository-owned functional contract for `GARDEN-WEB-PLAN-001`.
The complete scope remains 43 features and 210 work items: 196 current public/research items, and 14 conditional governmental items. The baseline specifications are not completed functionality.

## Responsibilities and people

The Garden must explain its proposal clearly and humanely, preserve accountable historical evidence, and support sustained collaboration. A public visitor can understand what is proposed, why its proponents consider it important, the funding design and risks, and how to investigate the evidence without signing in. ADOS and other descendant participants have substantive contribution, representation-challenge and privacy rights, not merely a promotional audience role.

[Feature ownership](feature-contract.json) assigns every F01-F43 function an accountable role and its full-feature acceptance release. Roles describe responsibility, not an assertion that staff have been hired or specialists have reviewed the work. A release label is not an exclusive implementation window; partial feature work can ship earlier only when its own gates pass. The release dependency closures in `plan/plan.json` remain authoritative.

## Research independence

A researcher, contributor or reader need not endorse the policy. Historical findings must not be edited to improve an advocacy argument. Preserve counterevidence, uncertainty, alternative identifications and review history. Source integrity does not prove a historical assertion. Authenticity, transcription accuracy, interpretation, rights clearance and permission to publish are separate decisions.

Families may permit research preservation without public display or endorsement. Do not use family records, race, ancestry, religion, inferred political beliefs or research behavior to target political persuasion. General public explanation and voluntary participation must not become sensitive-trait targeting.

## Public explanation and financial honesty

Separate proposed institutions, existing law, design intentions, illustrative scenarios and observed results. Explain intended recovery of public founding expenditures, the repayment mechanism and public exposure. An unqualified zero-taxpayer-cost claim is not authorized by the current draft. Do not describe an expected return as guaranteed repayment. The relevant source record and review requirements must accompany published fiscal material.

Historical communities are represented through institutions and full human lives, including continuity, resistance and change. Generated imagery must be labeled artistic reconstruction or conceptual illustration and never treated as archival evidence. Similarity of pose does not establish historical identity or relationship. Publication requires identity/context, historical and rights review.

## Authority and consent

An account is not citizenship or an eligibility decision. Interest registration is not official enrollment. No website feature confers governmental recognition, awards commercial rights, operates regulated enterprises or conducts binding governmental elections. F42-F43 and GDN-197-GDN-210 require actual legal authority, an authorized operator and separately assessed security before activation.

## Delivery and acceptance

Product comprehension, scholarly review, engineering checks, PR merge, deployment and production verification have distinct evidence. A merged documentation PR does not certify security, establish historical accuracy or operate a government program. Record exact commits, checks, review scope, unresolved gates and live verification separately. Do not manufacture independent reviewers or participant studies.

Follow [standing instructions](../standing-instructions.md) and [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md). Work autonomously on dependency-ready tasks, fix ordinary failures, and merge only after the latest required checks and GitHub mergeability permit it. No bypass of required review, branch rules or cost authorization is allowed. Missing credentials or specialist review block only the affected work; preserve explicit gates and continue independent work.

## Durable handoff

[Current status](../CURRENT_STATUS.md) and `plan/execution.json` record observations after the immutable planning snapshot. A new developer begins with the repository, not conversation history. Save changes in PRs, update evidence and integration mappings, retain prior decisions and never commit secrets or restricted research bytes.

## Revision and rollback

This charter operationalizes the approved scope; it does not change denominators or relax acceptance. Changes require a scoped PR, reasoned history and updated contract tests. Revert by a new commit with a correction record, never by erasing source or decision history.

# GDN-006 — Public information architecture and design studies

## Central promise
The Garden's opening must be legible in one sentence, humane in presentation, and backed by inspectable evidence. People arrive with unequal historical background; do not force sign-in, media playback, a donation form or a political commitment before learning.

## Primary navigation
The Garden · How it works · History & Atlas · Research · Participate · About. Permanent contextual links lead to Funding & risk, Questions and objections, the full proposed Act, reviewed records, and documented limitations. Private researcher collaboration lives beyond a distinct sign-in boundary.

## Journey: newcomer
Start with a short, human explanation of voluntary descendant-founded nations and why slavery's destruction of self-government and intergenerational economic capacity matters. Then a six-step mechanics guide: proposed law, historical census with corrections, voluntary affiliation, nation constitutions, state-level negotiated opportunities, independent accounting. Illustrative cases are labeled. Finish with a plainly written source and rights FAQ.

## Journey: descendant or family-history contributor
Start with the project's purpose and surviving community histories, not only violence. Provide clear paths to contribute or correct a record, receive attribution, restrict material or submit confidential feedback. Explain that contributing does not mean endorsing the policy, consenting to marketing, joining a nation, or proving eligibility. Require informed consent before any family record collection.

## Journey: skeptical policy reader
Offer the bill, independent legal questions, consent safeguards, a transparent fiscal framework with possible unrecovered public expenditures, and difficult objections without adversarial framing. The proposed financing is designed to recoup founding costs but repayment is contingent; do not claim categorical zero taxpayer cost.

## Journey: independent historical researcher
Public search first, evidence states visible (draft vs reviewed vs corrected), every claim links to underlying sources. Sign-in only for collaborative editing, restricted materials or saved private work. Discover existing archives, naming variants, confidence, disputed records and gaps.

## Visual grammar
Use editorial typography, restrained earth/ink tones, open whitespace, generous responsive grids and maps/timelines only when sources support them. Show full human lives and cultures before suffering. Display generated illustrations only with visible historical-reconstruction labels and reviewed permissions. No unlabeled archive-looking synthetic imagery, invented quotations or staged testimonials.

## Navigation acceptance
Keyboard skip link; semantic landmarks; links don't rely on JavaScript; form/status states plainly labelled; focus indicators visible; reduced motion; no audio autoplay; mobile navigation accessible; low-bandwidth text survives absent media. Avoid a modal/splash blocking the policy explanation.

## Study protocol (not yet conducted)
Recruit diverse voluntary participants without targeting or inferring political views: newcomer, descendant contributor, skeptical reader, independent researcher. Give each a scenario and ask participants to locate the program mechanics, financial caveat, an inspected primary source and research-contribution/privacy controls. Record task success, confusion, accessibility friction and proposed revisions without storing unnecessary demographic identifiers. Moderated comprehension, keyboard audits and real-device tests are future gates, not claimed as completed.

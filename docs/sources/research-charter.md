# Not From Nowhere — online research completion charter

Version 1.0 · adopted for execution 25 September 2026

## Goal and boundary

Produce a comprehensively searched, claim-audited, online-evidence edition of the atlas, not a claim to have recovered every historical life or every surviving record. The six-file Working Research Edition 1.18-alpha is the preserved starting point. No existing edition is overwritten. The research corpus is a separate control layer until an audited successor edition is ready.

The first completion cutoff is 25 September 2026. Records discoverable by that date form the primary census; later discoveries enter a dated supplement and may reopen a closed question. A cutoff is not evidence that all earlier material was found. Completion requires the documented searches and closure tests below, not elapsed time, page count, a quota of sources, or repeated unsuccessful searches alone.

## 1. Scope

- African political communities, nations, kingdoms, confederations, towns, institutions and peoples affected by slave trades; their histories before capture, political change, survival and descendants. Include named entities, genuinely evidenced unnamed polities, and separately classified unresolved origin evidence.
- Africa in its full geographic breadth: North Africa/Sahara; Senegambia/Upper Guinea; Gold Coast/Bights of Benin and Biafra; West-Central Africa; Sahel/Lake Chad; Nile/Horn; East African coast/interior/Great Lakes; Southern Africa; Madagascar and western Indian Ocean islands. These are search regions, not historical nations or mutually exclusive origin categories.
- Transatlantic, intra-American, trans-Saharan, Mediterranean, Red Sea and Indian Ocean trades, and internal African captivity and trading systems. Examine how systems interconnect without merging populations or counting the same movement twice.
- Earliest relevant documented antecedents through the twentieth century, with contemporary survival, remembrance and public descendant research through the cutoff. Period bins organize work; they do not imply equivalent evidence density or uninterrupted institutions.
- Destinations in the Americas, Europe/Mediterranean, North Africa, Arabia/Persian Gulf, South Asia and Indian Ocean islands, alongside internal African routes and return/resettlement. The book's American-descendant audience does not justify excluding other systems from the comparative research census.
- Lives and agency: family, kinship, gender and age, occupations, belief, language, education, law, capture, routes, sale, resistance, marronage, liberation, recaptivity, migration, community formation, survival and memory. Avoid an enslaver-only or administrative-only account.
- Online materials include digitized manuscripts, records, objects, maps, archaeology reports, published testimony and previously recorded oral histories, newspapers, theses, scholarly publications, datasets and their documentation. Published oral testimony requires collector, context, original language, date, consent/rights information where available; no new interviews are undertaken.
- Exclude new interviews, physical visits, fresh physical examination, private family contacts, new DNA collection and unsolicited contact with descendants. Catalog-only physical holdings belong in a deferred-research register, not a completed online-evidence claim.
- Free accounts and subscriptions are online but access-constrained. Use only authorized existing access. Do not buy, create accounts, accept new contractual terms, bypass limits, defeat access checks, contact repositories or publish private information without appropriate authority.

## 2. Units and identity rules

Maintain distinct types: person; family/household; polity; people/ethnolinguistic community; institution; place/port; vessel; voyage/event; archival object; archival witness; publication; derivative; assertion. A language, port, personal name, colonial category, institution or photograph is not a nation. A named umbrella pending split is not a final single polity.

For each identity store the exact recorded form, language/script, asserted time/place, recording agent, source locator, normalized search form, variant relationship and uncertainty. Preserve dated state changes, predecessor/successor relations, overlapping jurisdictions and disputed equivalences. Never merge same-name people, repeated sitters, editions, translations or repeated register entries automatically.

Named and unnamed polity sets must not overlap. A personal name with no origin does not prove an unnamed polity. An unnamed polity requires positive collective/political evidence, not merely a blank origin field. Promotion to a named record preserves the old evidence and an explicit supersession link. Scientific geographic assignments remain probabilistic and separate from personal identity and named-nation evidence.

## 3. Evidence and assertion controls

Every included factual assertion must become an atomic, traceable claim with exact wording, scope, dates, entity IDs, provenance, primary locator, supporting/contradicting evidence and an adjudication. Paragraph imports are only candidate units; a URL appearing beside a claim is not proof. A gateway is not an inspected item; an abstract is not full-text review; OCR is not a diplomatic transcription; a database is not an independent witness to the document it copies.

Record both evidence class and review state. Evidence classes: archival item; contemporary published witness; material/archaeological evidence; later testimony/oral history; scholarly interpretation; statistical estimate/model; repository metadata; discovery-only lead. Review states: unreviewed, partially inspected, supported-with-scope, contested, unsupported, withdrawn, access-blocked, deferred-offline, or closed-with-limits. No single confidence letter substitutes for source criticism.

Separate political existence, outcome, and causation. Destruction, conquest, collapse, severe extraction, transformation, dispersal and survival require distinct claims. Examine chronology and rival explanations; involvement in slavery does not establish destruction by slavery. Transcribe historical categories as evidence of a recorder's classification, not automatically as a person's self-identification.

Genealogical links require independently evaluated records, chronology, places, age ranges, relationships and conflicting candidates. Repeated web copies of one source are one evidence lineage. Do not identify living descendants or make kinship links from name, appearance or broad genetic/geographic affinity. Public trees are leads unless the underlying sources establish the link.

## 4. Search and coverage protocol

For every research lane: identify relevant repositories and scholarship; use catalog and full-text searches; search dated names, exonyms, endonyms, historical spellings and source-language terms; trace bibliographies backward and later citations forward; inspect finding aids and collection hierarchy; descend to record/folio/image; record queries, date, endpoint, results, access and exact work performed.

Search language plan includes English, French, Portuguese, Spanish, Dutch, German, Italian and Arabic, plus relevant African languages and Ajami scripts, Danish/Swedish and other archival languages as justified by holdings. Automated translation is an aid, not verified translation. Difficult readings remain flagged for a qualified second reading; no invented fluency or normalized manuscript text.

Coverage is multidimensional: African region × period × trade system × destination × evidence type. Geography assignments are discovery aids until reviewed. The initial matrix intentionally marks all research lanes not assessed; keyword incidence cannot close a lane. An irrelevant combination must receive a reasoned not-applicable decision, not disappear from the matrix.

For each important sequence, log known extent, scanned extent, accessible extent, ingested extent, directly inspected extent, transcribed extent, independently checked extent and missing leaves. Material conclusions cannot rest on unreported sampling. If a corpus is too large to exhaust, declare the sampling/extraction design and its limitations; do not label all records reviewed. Repeated registration counts are not unique-person counts.

Lane closure requires a completed repository census, all relevant in-scope hits triaged, material citations followed to evidence or a classified barrier, source-language/variant searches, explicit treatment of gaps, and an adversarial second search using different terms and repository routes. No new material finding in that second sweep supports closure but does not prove universal exhaustiveness. Material discoveries reopen affected lanes.

## 5. Visual and publication controls

For every embedded image retain: creator and role; exact title; dates and their meaning; repository; collection/call/reproduction/digital IDs; stable item URL; exact image URL; rights statement and access date; local filename; source and derivative SHA-256; transformations including crop coordinates; caption and inference limit; alternative text; permitted argument; pricing and publication next action. Use the exact note “Cost could not be ascertained.” when no price is established. Free access does not establish permission to reproduce.

Keep originals, alternate exposures, verso, composite page, later reproduction, detail crop and edited derivative distinct. Identify rulers, targets and capture equipment as modern digitization apparatus. Generated/reconstructed art must be explicitly segregated from archival evidence and never authenticate an historical appearance. Pose comparisons require a defined supported argument; resemblance cannot establish identity or ancestry.

## 6. Ten completion gates

| Gate | Required evidence of completion |
|---|---|
| 1. Charter | Operational scope, definitions, evidence rules, access boundary and stopping rules recorded. Complete at foundational release; revisable with a change log. |
| 2. Corpus | All six baselines accounted for; authoritative entities, sources, atomic claims and placements reconciled; all imports trace to bytes/locators; no silent merges. Ingestion alone is insufficient. |
| 3. Coverage | Every region/system/time/destination/evidence lane assessed with evidence or explicit reasoned gap, and disproportionate coverage addressed. |
| 4. Source census | Multilingual repository and scholarship searches completed, query logs kept, backward/forward citations resolved or barriers classified, final adversarial sweep recorded. |
| 5. Entity dossiers | Every accepted polity has dated existence, institutions, source criticism, outcome/causation and survival dossier; candidate umbrellas and unnamed records adjudicated; nonoverlap tested. |
| 6. Record sequences | Material sequences completed to the documented online extent, or explicitly bounded with missingness/sampling/access limitations and claims narrowed accordingly. |
| 7. Claims | Every included factual assertion reviewed; high-risk identity/causation/quantity claims checked; contradictions retained; unsupported claims removed, qualified or visibly unresolved. |
| 8. Images | Every placement has verified identity, provenance, relevance, rights/publication status and accessibility; every derivative is controlled; unresolved visuals withheld or explicitly qualified. |
| 9. Editorial synthesis | Main narrative and five companions synchronized; corrections integrated into current prose, not stranded in appendices; indexes, cross-references, maps and evidence access routes verified. |
| 10. Release QA | All gates signed off or explicitly reported as incomplete; actual rendered-page visual review logged for every final page; structural, relationship, provenance and accessibility checks; all editions preserved and recoverable. |

“Complete with online limitations” is permissible only with a public limitations report. Access-blocked and unreviewed are different. A barrier may narrow a claim; it cannot turn an unsupported claim into a verified one. No percentage of worldwide historical truth will be reported.

## 7. Execution priorities and handoff

First ingest and validate; then resolve source-code definitions and current versus superseded corrections; audit high-risk nation/causation/number claims; expand neglected regions and local-language testimony in parallel with the existing Marinerito/LOC queues. Each session must improve evidence or close a precisely defined task, save its checkpoint, and state what was not done. Do not endlessly grow the book with minor appendices while the core claims remain unaudited.

Current baseline-reported queues: 19 LOC-VIS objects; Marinerito rows 152–376; exact LOC-VIS-083 identity/call/reproduction/rights; Hawkins Wilson M1913 roll 58/volume 116; Harris M1875 roll 4; complete Wang et al. methods/supplement review. These are not the full project backlog. Preserve all uncertain readings and unverified modern links.

The next atlas edition requires material evidence changes, complete six-file synchronization and the final-page requirements above. The control corpus can advance independently without generating cosmetic atlas editions.

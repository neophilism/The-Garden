# GDN-007 — Editorial, expert, and image commissioning plan

The public site needs a persuasive but evidence-accountable presentation, not an archive dump or political-targeting funnel. Every high-stakes historical, legal, funding and rights claim has an identifiable qualified reviewer role and must be released only with an actual review record. A role in this plan is **not** a hired or verified reviewer.

## Content commissioning
Landing orientation; full lives and societies; mechanisms and conditional stages; voluntary affiliation and rights; financing and its possible public unrecovered expenditures; objections and comparisons; researcher independence and source trust; family history consent; organizational accountability. See `content-deliverables.json` for ten page deliverables and required reviews.

## Image treatment
Archival photographs/objects, generated historical reconstruction, conceptual art and family submissions use **four distinct** asset workflows. Generated art must be clearly labeled and never substituted for original historical evidence. The user's earlier reported portrait identity and grain-scale placement errors are explicit reasons to require identity and physical-accuracy checks. Visual pairing is acceptable only without implying linked identities or events. Untested assets must not be shown in the public build.

## Pre-publication gates
Legal counsel: verify draft-bill characterization, nature of self-government and eligibility limits, and unresolved constitutional powers.
Fiscal analyst: verify repayment mechanism, cost exposure, low-revenue scenarios, no categorical taxpayer-zero guarantee.
Historian and language reviewers: sources, nation/polity distinctions, uncertainty, independent alternative interpretations.
Rights specialist: actual image and quotation licenses, creator and source, required restrictions.
Community advisers: representation, safeguarding, revocable permissions where feasible and concerns handled without political profile targeting.
Accessibility: transcript, captions, semantics, contrast, keyboard, reduced motion, alternate text.

## Consent and independence
Research preservation, public display, attribution and advocacy reuse are separately revocable choices subject to disclosed archival and legal constraints. No ancestry or political-view inferred targeting. A person can challenge historical interpretation without endorsing the policy.

# ADR-011: Autonomous merges within actual repository policy

Status: proposed planning decision; verify integration-specific assumptions at implementation.

## Decision

Use protected branches and merge queue where supported, exact-head required checks, independent reviews where required and automatic next-ready work.

## Rationale

Autonomy is not bypass authority. A different account must be able to inspect why a merge was permitted.

## Review trigger and limits

Account entitlement and rules must be verified; fallback to serialized checked-head merges, not weakened protection.

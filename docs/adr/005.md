# ADR-005: Immutable raw corpus and versioned normalization

Status: proposed planning decision; verify integration-specific assumptions at implementation.

## Decision

Preserve CP-017 source bytes and every table in immutable staging. Normalize through explicit mapping and reversible lineage, never by replaying old scripts over the recovered DB.

## Rationale

Prior archives contain documented truncation and incomplete recovery. Byte integrity does not recover unknown state or validate historical claims.

## Review trigger and limits

Review each new checkpoint against its own manifest; use import-run identity and checksums to avoid duplicate or stale overlays.

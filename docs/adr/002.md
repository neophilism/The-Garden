# ADR-002: Public projection instead of querying restricted research

Status: proposed planning decision; verify integration-specific assumptions at implementation.

## Decision

Materialize publication-eligible fields and versions into a public-only serving projection. Authoritative research remains private; publication requires independent evidence, rights, consent and accessibility states.

## Rationale

It is easier to test what public infrastructure cannot read. Cache invalidation alone is insufficient; revocation denial must apply at origin and derivative delivery.

## Review trigger and limits

Review before federation or third-party indexing. Historical snapshot access still respects later privacy/rights restrictions.

# ADR-001: Modular monorepo with separate trust boundaries

Status: proposed planning decision; verify integration-specific assumptions at implementation.

## Decision

Choose a modular TypeScript monorepo for one Garden product, with separately deployable public and research apps, workers and domain packages. Keep government extensions in a separate repo/product. Public runtime uses only public projection credentials.

## Rationale

Shared models reduce drift while process/credential separation reduces unintended access. A public marketing site and private research platform may share UI primitives, not secrets or unrestricted database access.

## Review trigger and limits

Review if operational scale or legal tenancy requires service extraction; do not introduce microservices without a measurable boundary or scaling need.

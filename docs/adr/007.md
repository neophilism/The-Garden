# ADR-007: Early reviewed public release before full research platform

Status: proposed planning decision; verify integration-specific assumptions at implementation.

## Decision

Release high-quality public explanations, funding, legislative library and a reviewed evidence pilot under GDN-192. Do not wait for full corpus normalization, advanced collaboration or AI.

## Rationale

The public face is a primary requirement. Safe bounded publication is compatible with ongoing research when scope is honestly labeled.

## Review trigger and limits

R1 cannot bypass baseline security, rights, source/copy review, accessibility or real deployment verification.

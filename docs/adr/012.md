# ADR-012: No public claims beyond actual evidence or authority

Status: proposed planning decision; verify integration-specific assumptions at implementation.

## Decision

Separate policy advocacy from research conclusions; describe fiscal recovery as intended and expose remaining costs/risks. Government functions require enacted authority, dedicated operator and independent acceptance.

## Rationale

The current draft explicitly disallows a zero-fiscal-cost claim based only on intended repayment. Site accounts do not confer citizenship.

## Review trigger and limits

Update from actual reviewed legislation and fiscal assessment, never infer enactment from the date or a working prototype.

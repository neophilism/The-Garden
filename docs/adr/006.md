# ADR-006: Independent status axes and review scope

Status: proposed planning decision; verify integration-specific assumptions at implementation.

## Decision

Separate source acquisition, transcription, evidentiary review, rights, consent, accessibility, publication and dispute state. Link every substantive claim to exact evidence and every public placement.

## Rationale

A simple verified flag would silently imply too much. Item/page/row scope and source independence matter more than count of citations.

## Review trigger and limits

Review vocabulary with scholars and community advisers; preserve legacy statuses and mapping decisions.

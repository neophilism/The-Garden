# Incident handling

Separate operational outage, security/privacy exposure, research correction, image-rights withdrawal and provider failure. Each has an owner and escalation path. No real on-call staffing is asserted by this document.

For suspected unauthorized disclosure, deny affected delivery, suspend risky jobs, preserve evidence, restrict case access and rotate compromised credentials where appropriate. Do not indiscriminately erase audit evidence or publish private incident details. Determine impact with authorized security/privacy/legal reviewers; notification obligations depend on actual facts and applicable law.

For a material historical error, hold affected claims/placements, preserve old versions internally, assign independent review and trace all derivatives. Publish a reasoned correction without redisclosing information removed for privacy or rights.

For a failed import or corrupted backup, preserve exact bytes and validation records, stop promotion, reconcile from verified sources and record non-equivalence. Do not rerun historic scripts or claim a reconstruction is exact without evidence.

For vendor/AI/email/payment outage, fail safely, queue idempotent authorized work where appropriate and keep core reading/search available. Never compensate by sending sensitive data to an unapproved provider or incurring new costs without authority.

Record actual impact, timeline, containment, recovery, communication and corrective actions. Reopen relevant plan items and update Engine Room only with real evidence. Test alerts and ownership before claiming production monitoring.

Refer to the owner's current index `/guide_Not-From-Nowhere_LATEST.html` and `/Not-From-Nowhere_Current-Documents.json` through authorized access. The source manifest lists package names, hashes, sizes and the recovery boundary. Do not concatenate or regenerate an archive, replay older migrations, infer that preserved source rows are people, or import into a hosted service from this manifest.

## Required publication gates

Require documented research review and independent evidence, accurate captions and identities, rights clearance for images and source texts, family permissions where relevant, community safety, privacy/security assessment and accessibility review. Distinguish original evidence, transcription, contested assertions, editorial publication and political proposal. Follow [research charter](../../docs/sources/research-charter.md), [independent review](../../docs/research/scoped-review-states.md), [editorial review](../../docs/editorial/commissioning-and-review.md) and [offline staging rules](../../docs/adr/0014-offline-corpus-staging.md).
